## Introduction

WhatsApp is a messaging application owned by Meta Platforms that connects people around the world.

This guide describes how to set up WhatsApp, explore its interface, and use its key features. The guide shows you how to use Status, Calls, and WhatsApp Pay.

This guide helps you:

- Set up WhatsApp and your profile
- Explore the WhatsApp interface
- Use core features such as Status and Calls
- Use WhatsApp Pay to send money to a contact

### About WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a messaging app that uses your phone number to connect you with your contacts. You can send text messages, make voice and video calls, share photos, videos, and files. WhatsApp uses Wi-Fi or mobile data instead of your carrier's SMS or calling plan.

Every message and call on WhatsApp is end-to-end encrypted, which means only you and the person you communicate with can read or listen to them. WhatsApp cannot see the content in your messages or calls.

### About This Guide

This guide is written for new and existing WhatsApp users who want a reference for setting up and using WhatsApp. It covers first-time set up, basic interface, core features and WhatsApp Pay.

## Getting Started

Before you begin, make sure you have a smartphone that is Android or iOS, a phone number that receives SMS or phone calls, and has an internet connection.

### Register

#### Verify Your Phone Number

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Agree and Continue**.
2. Enter your country code and phone number, then tap **Next**.
3. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your phone.

*WhatsApp verifies your phone number and opens the profile setup screen.*

#### Set Up Your Profile

1. On the profile setup screen, enter your name.
2. Optional: Tap the **Camera** icon to add a profile photo.
3. Tap **Next**.

*Your profile is created, and WhatsApp opens to the Chats tab.*

### About WhatsApp Status

WhatsApp Status allows you to share photos, videos, and text messages. It updates your contacts throughout the day. Updates stay visible for up to 24 hours and then disappear automatically. Your contacts can view and react to your Status from the Updates screen.

#### Features of WhatsApp Status

**WhatsApp Status offers three types of updates:**

- **Photo Status** – Share an image from your camera or gallery.
- **Video Status** – Share a short video clip.
- **Text Status** – Share a short customizable message.

Contacts can react to any Status with a private message or send an emoji.

**Status Privacy Settings**

WhatsApp Status lets you share your Status with all your contacts. You can choose to select contacts to view your updates. Those who are not selected do not have access to your Status cannot not see it.

**Benefits of Using Status**

You can share moments with your contacts without needing to send individual messages. You can also get responses from your contacts. Updates disappear after 24 hours, keeping your profile current without needing to delete them manually.

### Update WhatsApp Status

This topic explains how to add a photo, video, or text to your Status.

**Prerequisites**

- You must have an active WhatsApp account.
- You must allow WhatsApp permission to access your gallery or your camera.

**Follow These Steps**

1. Open WhatsApp on your mobile device.
2. Select the **Updates** tab at the bottom left of your screen.
3. Tap **Add Status**.
4. Select **Camera** to take a photo or a video, or select from your **Gallery**.
5. Enter a caption if you wish.
6. Tap the green arrow on the bottom right to post your Status.

Your Status appears on the **Updates** screen for 24 hours. Contacts you did not select cannot view your update.

## Working with the Solutions

This chapter covers WhatsApp Pay, a solution that is separate from WhatsApp's core messaging features.

### WhatsApp Pay

#### Introduction

WhatsApp Pay lets you send and receive money directly within a chat, using a linked bank account. It is available only in select countries, including India.

#### Send Money to a Contact

Before you begin, link a bank account and set up a UPI PIN from **Settings** > **Payments**.

1. Open a chat with the contact, then tap the **Attachment** icon.
2. Tap **Payment**, enter the amount, then tap **Send**.
3. Enter your UPI PIN to confirm.

*WhatsApp sends the payment and shows a confirmation message in the chat.*

## Troubleshooting

### Photo status fails to post

**Problem**: The photo does not appear in My Status after you tap Send.

**Cause**: Weak or unstable internet connection.

**Solution**: Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection, then post the photo again.

## FAQs

### Is WhatsApp free to use?

Yes. WhatsApp is free to download and use, though your mobile carrier's data rates may apply.

### Can I use WhatsApp on more than one device?

Yes. You can link up to four devices to your account from settings.

### Can I use WhatsApp without a phone number?

No. You must have a valid, active mobile phone number to create an account.

## Best Practices

- Enable notifications so you do not miss new messages
- Add a profile photo so your contacts can recognize you
- Confirm your internet connection before starting a video call
- Check your Status privacy setting before posting anything sensitive
