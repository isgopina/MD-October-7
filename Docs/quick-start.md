## Overview

WhatsApp Status lets you share photos with your contacts. The photo disappears after 24 hours.

This guide helps you:

1. Open the status editor
2. Add and edit a photo
3. Post the photo to your status

## Before You Begin

- You need a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
- You will need a photo saved on your device, or permit WhatsApp to access your gallery.
- You will also need the latest version of WhatsApp.
